§ 00

A sky you can paint into.

Aether — from the Greek αἰθήρ, the luminous upper air and classical fifth element. The name carries the idea: you act upon the sky. There is no complex 3D editor here. A handful of gestures and sliders, and a sky that answers to real light.

§ 01

What it does.

A — Brush

Paint clouds with a finger.

Draw silhouettes on a simple canvas; a volumetric raymarcher turns each stroke into a real three-dimensional cloud, accumulated in a density field built from Perlin–Worley noise. Adjust brush radius and softness as you go.

B — Real light

Drag the hour; the sky follows.

The sun and a correctly phased moon are computed from the real place and instant. Move the hour slider — or let time advance on its own — and your clouds relight live, from warm dawn through white noon to lunar night.

C — Physical sky

Sun, moon, and ~9,000 stars.

An atmospheric sky (Rayleigh + Mie scattering) follows the sun. The moon shows its phase and earthshine; thousands of real stars from the Yale Bright Star Catalogue scintillate at night — all of it occluded by the clouds you paint.

D — Free gaze

Look anywhere. Keep painting.

Rotate the view with one finger and pinch to zoom — nothing erases. Paint in a new direction and a fresh cloud opens there, anchored in world space, so you can fill the whole sky around you.

E — Seascape

A sea that mirrors the sky.

On coastal landscapes, a ray-marched ocean stretches below the horizon — reflecting the sky, catching a glint of sun, and a path of moonlight at night.

F — Save a sky

Keep it as an .aether file.

Every sky saves to a self-contained file — landscape, clouds, place, hour and view. Reopen it any time and the exact image is recomputed from scratch. No cloud account, nothing to expire.

§ 02

From your finger to the sky.

A few strokes become a real, lit cloud. Drag the hour to relight it; look around to paint the whole sky. Captured on iPhone, nothing staged.

§ 04

Built with care.

A native Swift 6 and SwiftUI app, rendered by a Metal volumetric pipeline — raymarching, 3D noise, Henyey–Greenstein scattering and a powder effect — held at a smooth frame rate on iPhone through half-resolution tracing and temporal reprojection.

Solar and lunar positions come from astronomical formulae; the sky is physically motivated rather than a stock backdrop. Everything runs on device. Requires iOS 17 or later.

§ 05

Private by design.

·

Aether collects no data. No account, no server, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no ads. Location, if you allow it, is used only on your device to orient the sky — it never leaves the phone. Read the privacy policy.
